Tips for a good description

  • • List must-have skills separately from nice-to-haves so CV scoring can weigh them correctly.
  • • Name concrete tools, languages and frameworks instead of vague phrases like "modern stack".
  • • State the seniority and years of experience you expect.
  • • Describe the day-to-day work — it gives the interviewer realistic scenarios to probe.
  • • Mention soft skills that genuinely matter, such as stakeholder communication or mentoring.
